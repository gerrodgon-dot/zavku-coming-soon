Privacy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

ZAVKU is a financial technology platform developed by Grupo Financiero LR Soluciones, S.A.P.I. de C.V., Mexico.

This notice describes how information is handled on the ZAVKU institutional website. This website does not provide payment services or customer accounts.

Contact information

The contact form asks for your name, company, business email and message so ZAVKU can respond to institutional, technology and business inquiries. Please do not include financial details, identity documents or other sensitive information.

Message delivery is currently unavailable. Unsent message text stays in your browser unless you submit the form. Submissions are checked by the website service but are not stored in a contact database. While delivery is unavailable, submitted messages are not emailed or retained by the contact service.

Website operation

Technical information, including network addresses and request details, may be processed to deliver the website and protect it against abuse. The contact service uses a temporary, hashed network identifier to limit repeated submissions. This identifier expires after 10 minutes and is held in memory. Hosting infrastructure may maintain separate operational logs.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not add advertising cookies, analytics trackers or cross-site tracking scripts. Fonts and visual assets are served with the website.

Future contact delivery

This notice will be updated before message delivery is enabled, including the applicable retention information.

For privacy inquiries, contact contact@zavku.com.

Updates

Changes to this notice will appear on this page with an updated date.
